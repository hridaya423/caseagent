DAMP AND MOULD INSPECTION REPORT
Report ref: KW-2207-118
Date of inspection: 22 July 2026
Property: Flat 4, 18 Cedar Row, Willowbank, ZZ3 7ZZ
Instructed by: Northbridge Lettings    Tenant present: Yes (Ms Okafor)

1. SCOPE
Visual inspection and moisture meter readings of the rear bedroom (first floor) and adjoining wall faces. External wall inspected from ground level only.

2. FINDINGS
- Black mould growth to the north-east corner and the window reveal, approx 0.6 m2 in total.
- Moisture meter readings, internal wall face: 14-18% at the corner, 9-11% on other walls. Air humidity in room 71%.
- Clothes airer and damp laundry present in the room at time of visit.
- Trickle vent on the window found closed. No extractor fan in the room.
- No visible tide marks, salt deposits or plaster blowing consistent with penetrating damp at the time of inspection.
- External render and gutters viewed from ground level: no defects noted from this position.

3. OPINION
The mould is consistent with surface condensation caused by high indoor moisture, from drying clothes indoors, and insufficient ventilation. A building defect was not identified during this inspection.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS
a) Mould wash of affected areas with fungicidal treatment and anti-mould paint to the corner.
b) Occupant to avoid drying washing in the room, keep the trickle vent open and ventilate daily, and keep a consistent background heat.
