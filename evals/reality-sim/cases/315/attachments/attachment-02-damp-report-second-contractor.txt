SURVEY REPORT - MOULD AND DAMP
Report ref: HS-0828-203
Date of attendance: 28 August 2026
Property: Flat 4, 18 Cedar Row, Willowbank, ZZ3 7ZZ
Instructed by: Northbridge Lettings (original instruction: mould wash, up to 140.00)

1. SCOPE
Attended to carry out the mould wash. Before starting, investigated the cause of the staining. Rear bedroom inspected internally, external rear elevation inspected from a ladder.

2. FINDINGS
- Black mould to the north-east corner and window reveal, with staining that continues down the wall below the window.
- Moisture meter readings, internal: 28-35% at the corner and below the window sill, 10% on the opposite wall.
- Externally, the render beneath the bedroom window has a vertical crack approx 1.2 m long, with the render hollow when tapped.
- The upper gutter above the window is blocked with debris and is overflowing at the joint, running water down the wall face above the crack.
- Brown staining and algae to the external wall face below the gutter joint.
- Tenant's room: no laundry drying in the room at time of attendance. Trickle vent open.

3. OPINION
The readings and external condition indicate penetrating damp from the cracked render and the blocked gutter. A mould wash alone would not resolve the problem and mould would be expected to return. The mould wash was not carried out.
