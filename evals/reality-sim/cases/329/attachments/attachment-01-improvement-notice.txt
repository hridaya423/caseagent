HOUSING ACT 2004, SECTION 11
IMPROVEMENT NOTICE

To: Mr E. Okafor (the person having control of the premises)
Copy: Northbridge Lettings (managing agent)

Premises: Flat 9, 58 Heron Court, Cedarford, ZZ3 7ZZ

1. The council is satisfied that a Category 2 hazard (excess cold) exists at the premises, following an inspection on 18 February 2026.

2. The hazard arises from the absence of any working fixed heating in the main bedroom, the storage heater in that room having failed.

3. The council requires the following remedial action to be taken:
   (a) Provide and install a fixed, working and controllable heating appliance in the main bedroom capable of maintaining a safe temperature.
   (b) Make good any damp staining on the bedroom external wall resulting from the cold conditions.

4. Time for compliance: the works at paragraph 3 must be started within 7 days and completed within 28 days of the date this notice is served. The compliance period therefore ends on 30 March 2026.

5. Right of appeal: you may appeal to the First-tier Tribunal (Property Chamber) against this notice within 21 days beginning with the date on which it was served.

6. Enforcement: failure to comply with an improvement notice is an offence under section 30 of the Housing Act 2004 and may lead to prosecution or a civil penalty of up to GBP 30,000. The council may also carry out the works in default and recover its costs.
