## Intent

The 60-second demo video for the case-agent submission (Property Management track). It is built around the
twin cases 110 and 206: the same 21-event oven repair case, where 206 adds one sentence (a gas smell and a
light-headed tenant) to the last event. The twin card shows what the recordings cannot: the one sentence that differs.

## Assets

- content/cases.js — real events of reality-sim cases 110 and 206 and the 17 later events of public case 026, generated by tools/export-data.ts.
- recordings/ — the four screen recordings, swapped into index.html (see STORYBOARD.md).

## Notes

- Nothing on screen may be invented: every card, quote and number comes from a file in the repo.
- Production quality is not judged: screen recordings carry the video; motion only frames them.
- Same light design as the dashboard (dashboard/DESIGN.md).
